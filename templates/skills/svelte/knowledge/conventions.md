# svelte — Conventions

Naming and structural conventions for svelte.

---

- Use Svelte 5 runes ($state, $derived, $effect) over Svelte 4 stores
- Components PascalCase: `UserCard.svelte`
- SvelteKit routes use +page.svelte, +layout.svelte, +server.ts
- Prefer load() functions over client-side fetching

---

_Customize these conventions based on your team's preferences._
