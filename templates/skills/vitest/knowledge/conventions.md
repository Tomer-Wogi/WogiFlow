# vitest — Conventions

Naming and structural conventions for vitest.

---

- Compatible with Jest API — describe, it, expect work the same
- Use vi instead of jest: vi.mock, vi.fn, vi.spyOn
- Vitest is ESM-native — use import, not require
- Configure in vitest.config.ts or vite.config.ts test section

---

_Customize these conventions based on your team's preferences._
