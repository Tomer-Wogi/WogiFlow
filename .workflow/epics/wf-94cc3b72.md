**Priority**: P1

**Type**: epic
**Created**: 2026-04-15
**Source audit**: `.workflow/audits/2026-04-15-pre-igr-project.md` — C+ (78) baseline, adversary-verified

## Epic goal

Move the WogiFlow codebase from **C+ (78) → A (90+)** on the IGR-enhanced `/wogi-audit` rubric, measured against the same Gate 0 baseline and 7-dimension scoring used in the 2026-04-15 audit. A single re-audit at the end should produce a score of A or higher.

## Why A not A+

Gate 0 caps the overall score at 97 (penalty for the 2 lint-config downgrades). Fixing those downgrades (story 1 below) lifts the cap to 100. From there, the work that lifts the dimensions to A+ (94+) is a ~2x cost multiple vs the work that lifts to A (90). Diminishing returns kick in around A/A-. If A is reached early, individual stories can continue toward A+ on a case-by-case basis — but this epic's commit is A, not A+.

## Framing

- **Interpretation**: "Get us to A+" → close the 10 HIGH + top-priority MEDIUM findings from the pre-IGR audit; leave documented/acceptable LOWs. Re-audit to verify.
- **Scope in**: Each story targets one or more specific audit findings with evidence-tier traceability.
- **Scope out**: No new features, no new config keys beyond what existing findings mandate. No refactoring for aesthetic preference.
